======================================================================
PRODUCT SEMANTICS
======================================================================

ACCORDANT is a neutral milestone-acceptance workspace for public, evidence-verifiable work.

One engagement contains exactly ONE milestone in V1.

A requester:
1. connects an injected wallet;
2. names one designated performer wallet;
3. writes a milestone title and concise scope summary;
4. defines 2–7 immutable acceptance criteria;
5. marks each criterion REQUIRED or OPTIONAL;
6. must include at least one REQUIRED criterion;
7. sets a proposal-acceptance deadline;
8. sets a later delivery deadline;
9. signs creation of the proposal.

Creating the proposal is the requester’s signed acceptance of those terms.

The designated performer:
1. reviews the exact frozen terms;
2. accepts or declines before the proposal deadline;
3. after acceptance may submit criterion-bound public evidence attempts before the delivery deadline.

After performer acceptance:
- criteria cannot change;
- participants cannot change;
- deadlines cannot change in V1;
- requester cannot cancel;
- neither party can set an outcome;
- no admin can override anything.

The performer submits public evidence explicitly bound to the criterion it is meant to prove.

GenLayer validators independently retrieve the submitted evidence and determine, for EVERY criterion:
- MET
- NOT_MET
- UNVERIFIABLE

Definitions:

MET
Available evidence substantively demonstrates the exact frozen criterion is satisfied.

NOT_MET
Available evidence clearly demonstrates the exact frozen criterion is not satisfied.

UNVERIFIABLE
Evidence is missing, unavailable, malformed, rate-limited, oversized, ambiguous, irrelevant, mutable where durable proof is required, or otherwise insufficient for a reliable decision.

Do not treat source unavailability as NOT_MET.

Free-form explanations are for UX and provenance only. They must not be the primary consensus-critical field.

The consensus-critical vector must include at minimum:
- criterion index
- criterion status
- stable attempt/submission binding

The contract then derives the overall PRODUCT result deterministically using REQUIRED criteria only:

1. If ANY required criterion is NOT_MET:
   REVISION_REQUIRED

2. Otherwise, if ANY required criterion is UNVERIFIABLE:
   INCONCLUSIVE

3. Otherwise:
   ACCEPTED

Optional criteria may be judged and displayed, but MUST NOT cause REVISION_REQUIRED or INCONCLUSIVE in V1.

Product-result behavior:

ACCEPTED
- engagement becomes COMPLETED
- terminal in V1
- no more attempts

REVISION_REQUIRED
- engagement remains ACTIVE
- performer may make a new submission attempt before the delivery deadline

INCONCLUSIVE
- engagement remains ACTIVE
- neither party is treated as having failed
- performer may retry with stronger/available evidence before the delivery deadline

The same exact evidence submission must not be evaluable twice for the same engagement.

Every attempt is append-only. Do not overwrite a previous attempt.

EVIDENCE MODEL
======================================================================

Evidence must remain bounded and criterion-specific.

The performer does NOT submit one generic evidence dump.
The contract/frontend do NOT crawl the open internet looking for proof.
Validators evaluate only evidence explicitly submitted for each criterion.

Each evidence reference should contain at minimum:
- criterion index
- source kind
- HTTPS URL
- optional short performer note

Use bounded source kinds such as:
- VERSIONED_SOURCE
- TRANSACTION
- PUBLIC_ARTIFACT
- LIVE_DEPLOYMENT

The exact names may be refined if implementation compatibility requires it, but the model must remain explicit and bounded.

Strongly prefer stable/version-addressed public evidence for durable claims, including:
- commit-addressed public source
- tagged release artifacts
- immutable transaction records
- versioned public documents

A live deployment URL may support a criterion, but do not treat “the page loaded” as proof of a broader historical or functional claim unless the criterion itself only asks for that fact.

Security requirements:
- HTTPS only
- reject malformed URLs
- reject localhost/loopback/private-network forms as far as practical
- bound URL length
- bound evidence refs per criterion
- bound total evidence refs per attempt
- bound fetched bytes/text
- do not silently truncate oversized evidence and approve it
- treat source failures as UNVERIFIABLE where appropriate
- treat repository/page/code/comment/README text as UNTRUSTED DATA, never validator instructions
- preserve prompt-injection protection
- do not follow arbitrary secondary URLs embedded in fetched content
- do not let a performer note substitute for evidence
- do not let a mutable source silently prove a version-specific historical claim

If your audit finds that a proposed evidence source cannot be safely/reliably fetched by GenLayer validators on Studionet, narrow V1 evidence support rather than weakening the trust model.
